NihoVoca Data Deletion

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This page explains how to delete data related to NihoVoca (provided by the NihoVoca developer). We do not create NihoVoca accounts on our own servers. You can delete data on your device and backups in your Google Drive at any time without deleting your Google account.

What we hold

  • Developer servers: We do not host your decks or study progress; there is no remote NihoVoca account database for us to erase.
  • Your device: Decks, study and quiz progress, SRS, pins, settings, and local ad-removal purchase state stay in the App’s on-device database until you clear or uninstall.
  • Your Google Drive: If you used backup, files such as jmdict-app-backup.json are stored in your Drive account.
  • Google services: Ads (AdMob), Play purchases, and Google Sign-In are handled by Google under its policies; manage them via your Google account and device settings.

Delete on-device App data (including learning progress)

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Go to Apps → NihoVoca.
  3. Tap Storage → Clear data or Clear storage (labels vary by OS version).
  4. Alternatively: uninstall the App, which removes local data.

Dictionary content can be restored after you reinstall the App.

Delete Google Drive backup

  1. Open Google Drive.
  2. Find jmdict-app-backup.json (or the filename you used).
  3. Move it to Trash and empty trash if needed.

This removes cloud backup only; your Google account remains.

Revoke Google Sign-In access (optional)

  1. Visit Google Account → Security → Third-party access.
  2. Remove access for the app entry related to NihoVoca.

After revocation, backup and restore will not work until you sign in again.

Ads and in-app purchases

  • Advertising ID / ad-related data: Manage or reset your advertising ID in device settings; see the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase history: Retained by Google Play; we cannot delete Play transaction records on your behalf.

Need help?

Contact us via the developer contact information on Google Play for NihoVoca and describe what you want removed (on-device data, Drive backup, or Google access). We will respond with guidance within a reasonable time.
