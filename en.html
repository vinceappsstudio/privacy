NihoVoca Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy describes how the mobile application NihoVoca (“the App”) handles information related to you. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

1. Who we are

The App is provided by the NihoVoca developer (“we”, “us”). For questions about this policy, please contact us using the developer contact details listed on Google Play.

2. What we collect

We do not require you to create an account with us, and we do not host user profiles on our own servers.

  • Learning data on your device: custom word decks, study and quiz progress, spaced-repetition (SRS) state, pins, and settings (theme, language, pronunciation preferences, etc.) are stored primarily in a local database on your device.
  • Dictionary data: Japanese dictionary content is provided as read-only data bundled with or updated through the App; it does not identify you personally.
  • Data you choose to back up to Google Drive: if you use backup in Settings, after you authorize access we export deck and learning-related data to a JSON file (e.g. jmdict-app-backup.json) and upload it to your Google Drive. Backups do not include the full dictionary database. Backup is optional and initiated by you.
  • In-app purchase status: if you buy “remove ads” or similar products, Google Play (or the App Store on iOS, when available) processes payment; we only store purchase state locally to hide ads and may sync entitlements with the store on launch.

3. Third-party services

The App may use the following third parties, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: banner ads at the top of the App when you have not purchased ad removal. Google may collect device identifiers, advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data as described in the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob documentation.
  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive: used only when you back up or restore data, to authenticate you and access files you authorize. We do not log your OAuth tokens to our servers in application code.
  • Google Play Billing (Android): processes in-app purchases and restore purchases under Google’s policies.

4. How we use information

  • Provide lookup, study, quiz, and related features;
  • Play pronunciation using on-device text-to-speech according to your settings;
  • Back up or restore your learning data when you request it;
  • Show ads when you have not removed them, and hide banner ads after a valid purchase;
  • Maintain security and stability of the App (e.g. local diagnostics without intentionally uploading personal data to our servers).

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is processed only as needed to provide the features above, including processing by third parties you interact with (Google ads, sign-in, Play billing).

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local learning data remains on your device until you clear app data, uninstall the App, or overwrite local data via restore.
  • Google Drive backups remain in your Drive account until you delete them.
  • Ad and purchase-related data is retained by Google under its policies; you can limit ad personalization or manage your Google account in device and Google settings.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe we have received a child’s personal information in error, please contact us via the store listing.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures for on-device data handling. Network transfers (backup, ads, billing) rely on third-party security. Please protect your Google account and device.

9. Your choices

  • You may use the App without Google backup;
  • You may reset or limit ad tracking in device settings where supported;
  • You may purchase a one-time ad removal product (price shown in the store);
  • You may uninstall the App to remove local data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will revise the effective date on this page for material changes. Please review this page periodically.

11. Contact

Please contact us via the developer contact information on Google Play for NihoVoca. If you publish a dedicated support email in the store, you may add it here as well.

12. Data deletion and revoking access

We do not create NihoVoca accounts on our servers. You can delete on-device data, remove Drive backups, or revoke Google Sign-In access without deleting your Google account. See our dedicated page for step-by-step instructions: NihoVoca data deletion.
